## Design
The archive has a central hall with six independent views through arch-shaped doorways. A vector perspective floor, columns, central arch and celestial engraving provide depth; each aperture displays an existing room illustration, and its foreground object matches the destination. The center retains the standing character and rabbit. The entrance can reach the video shelf. The source video shelf still comes first. Mobile: center and two-column portals with native links, no hover-only information. Desktop: left and right three doors. New original vector assets (not image-generated) are archive-architecture.svg (1200×720) and archive-folio.svg (160×48); used by ArchiveAtlas. Six room artworks are reused from the approved manifest, not new generated images. No fictional diary or added reading content. 
Library: binding-edge accession entries with shelves and metadata rails. Words: numbered specimens in a drawer, clear usage and evidence counts. Concepts: classified files, visual distinctions by actual kind. Topics: coordinates and pips based only on actual document count (not similarity or confidence), direct source links unchanged. People: dossier-like monograms. Workspace: actual local collection label and related room art. Reader: subtle folio rule and annotation bracket only.
Motion: brief lamp reaction, 1.4s dust reveal, responsive portal light, finite changes; reduced-motion disables them. No loading overlay, forced transitions, persistent loop or delay.

# Website as authored work — research (2026-11-26)
Status: research only; no visual direction approved. Owner: 「インタラクティブというよりもっと自分の世界観や思想を打ち出してほしい」「全然違う。もっと作品的なサイトを研究して来いよ」. The Second Look eye/correction-mark prototype was rejected. Do not treat previous Imprint, Marginalia, Asterism, Fold, Palimpsest, or Second Look as accepted.
## Corpus: original sites and maker documentation
- Chia Amisola, Everything I Love https://everythingi.love/ ; https://amiso.la/site/ ; https://moha.wiki/Chia_Amisola_(Homepage_Project). Browser-window compositions, religious imagery, halftone, black/pink, fragments form an authored vocabulary tied to love, memory, belief and technology. Visual evidence: https://everythingi.love/cathedral.png (artwork, not necessarily homepage).
- The HTML Review https://thehtml.review/03/ ; https://thehtml.review/05/ ; https://thehtml.review/about . Issue 03 uses radial typography and floating entries; issue 05 uses a fish and layered index. The publication is a space for web-native literature; each edition can have a distinct composition. Screenshot from issue 03: https://thehtml.review/images/issue-03.png .
- David Whyte Experience https://immersive-g.com/projects/david-whyte-experience/ . Studio documents watercolor landscapes, poetry, sound and life narrative. Sparse text shares the canvas with the painting; no visual competition from controls. Study illustrative voice and quiet hierarchy, not forced scrolling or WebGL.
- The Boat https://distil.im/projects/the-boat ; https://www.matthuynh.com/stories/theboat-9rw43 ; https://www.commarts.com/project/23899/the-boat . Ink illustration, typography based on artist handwriting, archival photos and sound are integrated into the same narrative treatment. Artist reports 222 static illustrations and 59 animated sequences. This is a single story, not a scalable library: derive a reusable original asset grammar rather than copy its production budget.
- Cameron's World https://www.cameronsworld.net/ ; https://art.cameronaskin.com/ ; https://www.espaciobyte.org/camerons-world . Authored collage made of recovered GeoCities fragments. Aesthetics follow the archival subject, not a universal component kit. Do not copy vintage GIFs or sacrifice discoverability.
- herri https://herri.org.za/10/aryan-kaganof/ ; https://herri.org.za/about/ ; https://arte-util.org/projects/herri/ . Editor explains the screen-based genealogy, integrated multilingual paragraphs, cinema/game/art/music/text and relational philosophy in actual design. Important comparable project: bilingual archive and pedagogy are also aesthetic/epistemic choices. Do not import the author's political program as ours. Issue dates exist on current About despite a description of perpetual now.
- The Creative Independent https://thecreativeindependent.com/about/ ; https://laurelschwulst.com/e/tci-website/ . Maker describes evergreen multilingual network, interview as atomic unit, editorial highlights rather than pullquotes, linked metadata and evolving site. Spiral embodies returning to problems; snail imagery in historical screenshots, monospace, strong editorial color and straightforward index. Study recurring motif + functional content system, not snail branding.
- The Public Domain Review https://publicdomainreview.org/about/ . Curated strange/beautiful historical source material; self-described digital cabinet of curiosities, essays and themed collections; separate catalogue and infinite browsing modes. Material and taste become identity. Do not substitute decoration for source fidelity.
- Atlas of Places https://simonmalz.com/project/atlas-of-places-website/words/ ; https://www.simonmalz.com/project/atlas-of-places-website/ . Designer treats the collection as visual atlas and index, with thematic coordinates. Quiet counterexample: original selection and rhythm carry authorship even with conventional controls.
## Transfer hypotheses (not approved direction)
1. Form follows a distinct editorial worldview and source selection, not a collection of metaphors (library, star map, paper stock, eye, correction symbols). Design imagery and typography with actual content as the material.
2. Define an authored visual vocabulary of original imagery, graphic marks, typographic hierarchy and composition, before CSS or reusable components. No fictional logs or new narrative required. The existing character/art and thumbnails are assets to evaluate, not automatically discard or retain in every layout.
3. Study different scales: whole screen composition, one real bilingual reading spread, one actual index, and individual glyphs/labels/links. One expressive voice must survive a screenshot without animation or manifesto text. Reading/search/mobile translation/video controls remain accessible and usable.
4. Separate expressive entry/curation surfaces from quiet reading; same visual vocabulary across them, different density. Maintain actual source attribution and original thumbnails. No scroll hijack, gratuitous 3D, complex toggles, invented metadata, or arbitrary imagery.
5. Before changing the site, compare real source screens with an authored visual composition containing actual AW material, not isolated button samplers or generic UI templates. Evaluate subject specificity, image/typography quality, visual rhythm, distinctiveness and fit to content, separately from CI.
## Evidence limits
Web-accessible original project pages, maker descriptions and published images/screenshots reviewed; no claim of live browser interaction tests across all sites, source-code inspection, or user preference validation. No new production implementation and no acceptance claim.
